Prefer EHR push notifications

Our EHR now supports generic phone push alerts without Twilio. Open Encounter inbox → EHR push notifications. On iPhone or iPad, add the EHR to your Home Screen, open it there and enable notifications.

The SMS setup below is optional and requires Twilio capabilities that current trial accounts do not provide for custom alert messages. Twilio’s newer trials allow pre-defined messages to verified recipients; a direct From number does not remove those content restrictions. See current trial restrictions.

Connect your phone alerts

These steps activate the existing alert service. Credentials belong in Supabase’s server secrets. The EHR’s connection checklist shows what remains without displaying secret values.

1. Set up Twilio and the SMS sender

Open the Twilio console. Keep your Account SID (AC…) and Auth Token in your credential manager. Choose an SMS-capable sender and complete the applicable registration.

For this custom alert integration, use an upgraded account and an approved SMS sender. Paid accounts using US local ten-digit senders require the applicable A2P 10DLC registration. On trial, verify your recipient mobile in Twilio as well as in this app.

Messaging Service and sender instructions · Trial restrictions

2. Create the two services

In Twilio, open Messaging → Services → Create Messaging Service. Name it KidneyCare clinician alerts. Under Sender Pool → Add Senders, attach your approved SMS number. Save its MG… service SID.

Create a separate service in the Twilio Verify console named KidneyCare, with SMS verification enabled. Save its VA… SID. The default six-digit code works with the app. Verify sends codes; the Messaging Service sends encounter alerts.

3. Generate the contact encryption key locally

Run this in Windows PowerShell. It produces a random 32-byte key encoded as 64 hexadecimal characters. Save a secure backup and enter it only in the Supabase secret below. Preserve an existing key while saved contacts use it; replacing it requires phone re-verification.

$bytes = New-Object byte[] 32
$rng = [System.Security.Cryptography.RandomNumberGenerator]::Create()
$rng.GetBytes($bytes)
$rng.Dispose()
([System.BitConverter]::ToString($bytes)).Replace("-", "").ToLowerInvariant()

4. Save the six server settings

Open the Supabase project → Edge Functions → Secrets. Save these settings, setting SMS_DELIVERY_MODE last when the account, services and registered sender are ready.

Return to Encounter inbox → Phone alerts → Check connection status. The checklist checks presence and format; it does not prove valid credentials, sender registration or account balance. No redeployment is needed after saving secrets.

5. Verify your phone and opt in

  1. Sign in to the EHR and open Encounter inbox → Phone alerts, or say “Open SMS alerts.”
  2. Enter your US mobile as +1 followed by ten digits, acknowledge verification-message consent, and press Send verification code.
  3. Enter the code and press Verify phone.
  4. Choose Text my verified phone, select at least one alert type, acknowledge alert consent, and press Save alert preferences.
  5. Choose your time zone. Enabled quiet hours are 10 p.m.–7 a.m. in that zone.

Submitting the verification form sends a real verification code. Merely checking connection status does not send messages.

6. Confirm a real alert

Save corrected synthetic encounter text and let the orchestrator prepare a new draft. Leave the encounter ready for review. The minute scheduler sends eligible notifications, subject to quiet hours, the current job revision, a two-minute spacing limit, three per hour and ten per day.

Run alert preview always creates a preview; it never sends a text. A preview does not complete the real-delivery checklist step.

Check Recent alerts. Accepted and sent are distinct from delivered. A carrier-reported delivered receipt matching your saved preference version completes the checklist. If you change preferences or the phone, a new delivery test is needed.

SMS contains generic encounter status and a secure sign-in link. Patient names, lab results and note text stay in the EHR. Opening a link does not sign the encounter or approve clinical actions.

If something is blocked

  • A server setting is missing or invalid: check the exact name, SID prefix and value format in Supabase, then refresh the connection status.
  • The code does not arrive: check your number, trial verified-recipient list, Verify SMS settings, account balance and Verify logs.
  • No encounter alert arrives: check the Messaging Service sender pool and registration, saved phone verification and consent, selected alert types, quiet hours and Recent alerts.
  • The message says sent: wait for carrier status polling. Sent does not confirm delivery.
  • Stop alerts: choose Alerts off and save, remove your saved phone, or reply STOP to the Twilio sender.
